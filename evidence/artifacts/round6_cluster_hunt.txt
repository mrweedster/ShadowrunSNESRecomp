== verdict ==
  Of the 109 AOT bodies the rejected seed adds, demoting
  $80AD9F and $80C530 alone restores the shipped pair:
  crc32 identical and no non-label WRAM byte differs over 4575 frames.
  Removing either one from that set brings the divergence back, so the
  attribution is 1-minimal over the 52-body starting set. The
  disassembly and the write trace name the mechanism: $80C530
  latches the PPU counters ($2137 then $213C/$213D), mixes them into
  DP $00/$01 and $1D04, and the trace shows its compiled store
  writing $0001=82 against the interpreter's 08 at $80C54D.
  $80AD9F is the 73-instruction loop that reaches $80ABBC, round 4's
  rejected seed; demoting it takes that compiled path out as well.

  The frame count is not monotone in the demotion set: 305 frames with
  both compiled, 1409 with $80C530 interpreted, 2485 with $80AD9F
  interpreted, 0 with both interpreted. A single wrong value would move
  one byte at one point; this is the signature of a read whose result
  depends on when it runs. The seed is adoptable only with both bodies
  interpreted, and that keeps 3.4 % of its cycle gain: 143,873 cycles
  against 4,212,797 with the bodies compiled. It buys
  90 extra AOT bodies, 179 extra nodes and
  +0.074 points of AOT share for a change the
  gate cannot see.
  The next lever is not another seed: it is making the AOT path charge
  the same cycles at that counter read.
